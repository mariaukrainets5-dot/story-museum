# Episode 1 — The Great Fire of London (1666)

Storyteller: Samuel Pepys. Level: Year 1–3, short sentences, key words in **bold**.
Facts checked against standard KS1 sources: started 2 Sept 1666 in Thomas Farriner's bakery, Pudding Lane; lasted about 4 days; ~13,000 houses burned; firebreaks made by pulling down houses; Pepys buried his Parmesan cheese and wine in his garden; St Paul's rebuilt by Christopher Wren; we know from Pepys's diary.
